Support

We're here to help you get the most out of Klayr.

Contact us

Email us at Klayrapp@icloud.com for help with the app, questions about your account, or privacy requests. We aim to respond within 3–5 business days, although response times may vary.

To help us assist you faster, please include your device model, your iOS version, and a short description of what happened (and what you expected to happen).

Logging in and passwords

Klayr accounts use an email address and password. If you forget your password, use the password-reset option on the sign-in screen; a reset email will be sent to your address. If a reset email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder and confirm you used the same email you signed up with.

Circles

A Circle is a small group of people you choose to coordinate with. You can create a Circle or join one, and Circle members can see shared events and your profile name and photo. If a new member doesn't appear right away, make sure both people are connected to the internet, then leave the Circle screen and reopen it to reload the latest members.

Events

Create an event and choose its visibility: keep it Private, share it with your whole Circle, or use Invite People to pick specific members and add outside guests. Shared events sync to the people you share them with, and they can RSVP.

Invitations

When you invite someone who isn't a Klayr member, Klayr prepares the invitation and then you send it yourself using your iPhone's share sheet (for example, Messages or Mail). Klayr does not automatically send texts or emails on your behalf, so remember to tap Send for each guest. Only invite people who are okay hearing from you.

Notifications

To receive notifications, allow them for Klayr when prompted, or enable them in iOS Settings → Notifications → Klayr. Notifications depend on your device settings, Apple's systems, and your connection, so they can occasionally be delayed or missed. If notifications stop working, confirm they're enabled and that you're signed in.

Messages

You can send direct messages to members of your Circle. Messages are delivered to and visible to the people in the conversation. If you experience content that violates our Terms of Use, please report it to us at the email above.

Voice capture

Voice features need microphone and speech-recognition permission, which you can manage in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security. Your speech is transcribed to text (using Apple's speech recognition), and the text is then sorted into categories with the help of an AI provider. Klayr doesn't save your raw audio. If voice capture isn't working, check that both permissions are allowed.

Deleting your account

You can permanently delete your account in the app under Settings → Delete Account. This removes your account and its active associated data. Please note that content you shared with others may remain visible to those recipients, and limited residual copies may persist temporarily in routine backups or logs.

Privacy requests

For questions or requests about your information, email us at Klayrapp@icloud.com. See our Privacy Policy for details on what we collect and how it's used.
