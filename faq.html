Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about Klayr.

What is Klayr?

Klayr is a shared organization platform that helps families, friends, and groups stay connected through messaging, calendars, invitations, reminders, tasks, shopping lists, and availability sharing.

What is a circle?

A circle is a private group of people who share information and collaborate together inside Klayr.

How do I invite someone?

Create a circle and send an invitation code to the person you would like to invite.

Can I create more than one circle?

Yes. You can participate in multiple circles at the same time.

Does Klayr support direct messaging?

Yes. Klayr supports private messaging between members.

Can I share events and availability?

Yes. Users can share events, availability information, invitations, reminders, and RSVP responses.

Are my messages private?

Klayr takes reasonable steps to protect your information and shares data only as described in the Privacy Policy.

Can I delete my account?

Yes. Account deletion is available directly within the application.

What devices are supported?

Klayr is currently designed for Apple devices.

How do I contact support?

Please visit the Support page for assistance.
