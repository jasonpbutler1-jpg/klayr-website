Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the most common questions about Klayr.

What is Klayr?

Klayr is an iOS app that helps you organize your day and coordinate with a small group of people you choose. You can capture things by typing or speaking, and Klayr sorts them into categories like calendar, tasks, shopping, reminders, ideas, meals, and capture. You can also share events and message the people in your group.

What is a Circle?

A Circle is a small, private group — like a family or a close group of friends — that you choose to coordinate with. Members of a Circle can see events you share with the Circle and your profile name and photo, and can message one another.

How do shared events work?

When you create an event, you choose its visibility: Private (only you), your whole Circle, or specific people via Invite People. Shared events sync to the people you shared them with, and they can RSVP. You can also add "items to bring" for guests to claim.

How do outside invitations work?

You can invite people who aren't Klayr members by entering their email address or phone number. Klayr prepares the invitation, and then you send it yourself using your iPhone's share sheet — for example, through Messages or Mail. Klayr does not automatically send SMS or email messages on your behalf, so be sure to tap Send for each guest. Please only invite people who are okay hearing from you.

Is my data private? Do you sell it?

We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for targeted advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites. Some of your information stays only on your device, while information needed for shared features is stored on our service infrastructure so it can sync with the people you share with. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How does voice capture and AI sorting work?

With your permission, Klayr records what you say and transcribes it to text using Apple's speech recognition. The resulting text is then sent to a third-party AI provider (Anthropic) to suggest which category each item belongs in. Klayr doesn't keep your raw audio, and AI suggestions can occasionally be wrong, so it's a good idea to review them.

Why didn't I get a notification?

Notifications depend on your iOS notification settings, Apple's delivery systems, and your internet connection, so they can sometimes be delayed or missed. Make sure notifications are enabled for Klayr in iOS Settings → Notifications → Klayr, and that you're signed in. Please don't rely on notifications as your only way to track important events.

How do I delete my account?

Open the app and go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and its active associated data. Content you shared with others may remain visible to those recipients, and limited residual copies may persist temporarily in routine backups or logs.

How do I report a problem or abusive content?

Email us at Klayrapp@icloud.com with a description of the issue and, if possible, your device model and iOS version. Content that is abusive, harassing, or otherwise violates our Terms of Use is not allowed, and we appreciate reports.

Who can see my messages?

Direct messages are visible to the people in that conversation. Events and other content are visible to the people you choose to share them with.

Does Klayr work without an internet connection?

Your personal, unshared items are stored on your device and remain available offline. Shared features — such as Circles, shared events, invitations, messages, and notifications — need an internet connection to sync.
