Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2026

Introduction

Welcome to Klayr. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains what information Klayr collects, how it is used, how it is protected, and the choices available to you.

By using Klayr, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Profile photograph
  • Account preferences

User Content

  • Messages
  • Events
  • Calendar information
  • Availability information
  • Invitations and RSVP responses
  • Notes, reminders, tasks, and shopping items
  • Uploaded photos and other content

Device and Technical Information

  • Device type
  • Operating system version
  • Application version and build number
  • Push notification token
  • Diagnostic and error information

How We Use Information

We may use collected information to:

  • Create, maintain, and secure user accounts
  • Provide circles, direct messaging, shared events, invitations, and availability features
  • Deliver push notifications and service-related messages
  • Synchronize information between authorized users
  • Operate artificial-intelligence-assisted organization features
  • Diagnose technical problems and improve performance
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Comply with legal obligations

Artificial Intelligence Features

Klayr may use artificial intelligence to help categorize, organize, and interpret information submitted by users. Users should review AI-assisted results before relying on them. Klayr does not guarantee that AI-generated or AI-organized results will always be complete or accurate.

Sharing of Information

Klayr does not sell personal information.

Information may be shared:

  • With other users when you intentionally share messages, events, availability, invitations, or other content
  • With service providers that help operate Klayr
  • When required by law, court order, or valid legal process
  • To protect the rights, security, and safety of Klayr, its users, or others

Third-Party Services

Klayr may rely on third-party services to provide app functionality, hosting, authentication, notifications, and infrastructure. These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Current or anticipated service providers may include:

  • Apple
  • Supabase
  • Cloud hosting and infrastructure providers
  • Artificial intelligence service providers

Push Notifications

Klayr may send notifications relating to direct messages, invitations, event updates, RSVP activity, reminders, and account information. You may manage notification permissions through your device settings.

Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Klayr, maintain account functionality, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements. Some information may remain temporarily in backups after account deletion.

Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information. However, no electronic storage or transmission method is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights concerning your personal information. Klayr users may:

  • Review and update profile information
  • Change notification permissions
  • Delete their account from within the app
  • Request support regarding account or privacy concerns

Account Deletion

You may request account deletion through Klayr's Settings screen. Account deletion removes or de-identifies information associated with your account, subject to legal, security, technical, and backup-retention requirements.

Children's Privacy

Klayr is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that such information was collected, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

International Users

If you use Klayr outside the United States, your information may be processed in countries where Klayr or its service providers operate. Data-protection laws may differ from those in your country.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Klayr changes. The updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy may be submitted through the Klayr Support page.
