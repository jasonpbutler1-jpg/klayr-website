Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 6, 2026.

Klayr ("Klayr," "we," "us," or "our") is operated by Jason Butler, an individual. Klayr is not currently organized as an LLC or corporation. This Privacy Policy explains what information the Klayr iOS app collects, how it is used, who it is shared with, and the choices you have.

By using Klayr, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the app.

Quick summary

  • Klayr helps you organize personal items and coordinate events with a small group of people you choose (a "Circle").
  • We collect the information needed to run those features — your account details, the content you create, and a few device permissions you grant.
  • We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for targeted advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • Some information stays only on your device; other information is stored on our service infrastructure so shared features can work.
  • You can delete your account and its active data from within the app.

Information we collect

Account and profile

When you create an account you provide an email address, a password, and a display name. You may add a profile photo. Your password is handled by our authentication provider and is not visible to us in readable form.

Circle data

If you create or join a Circle, we store the Circle, its members, and each member's role so the group's shared features work.

Events and RSVPs

When you create a shared event, we store its details (such as title, notes, location, date and time, category, and any "items to bring" list) and the RSVP responses of the people invited.

Messages

Klayr includes direct messages and event- and Circle-related messages. The content of these messages is stored so it can be delivered to and displayed for the intended recipients.

Outside invitations

When you invite someone who is not yet a Klayr member, you enter that person's email address or phone number so the invitation can be prepared. Klayr stores that contact value together with the invitation. Klayr does not automatically send text messages or emails to that person — you send each invitation yourself using your iPhone's share sheet (for example, Messages or Mail). You are responsible for having permission to contact the people you invite.

Voice capture and speech recognition

If you use voice features and grant microphone and speech-recognition permission, Klayr captures audio to transcribe what you say. Transcription is performed using Apple's speech recognition, which may process the audio on Apple's systems. Klayr does not save your raw audio recordings; it uses the resulting text.

AI processing

To sort your captured or typed entries into categories (such as calendar, tasks, shopping, reminders, ideas, meals, or capture), the text is sent to a third-party AI provider, Anthropic, which processes the text to return suggested categories. Only the text needed for categorization is sent.

Photos and camera

If you set a profile photo, Klayr uses your camera or Apple's system photo picker to obtain the image you choose, and stores that image as your avatar. When you choose an existing photo, Klayr uses Apple's built-in photo picker and receives only the specific photo you select; it does not request access to your entire photo library.

Calendar access

If you grant calendar permission, Klayr can read your calendar to display your events and can add your Klayr events to your Apple Calendar. Your Apple Calendar data is handled on your device by Apple's system and is not copied to our servers by this feature.

Push notification tokens

If you enable notifications, Klayr stores a device push token so we can deliver notifications to your device. Notifications are delivered through Apple's Push Notification service, and the notification content (such as an event name or a sender's name) passes through Apple to reach your device.

Device permissions, in short

Klayr may ask for access to your microphone, speech recognition, camera, photo library (only the photo you pick), calendar, and notifications. You control these in your iOS Settings and can change them at any time. Klayr does not request location or contacts access, and does not use any advertising or cross-app tracking identifiers.

What is stored on your device vs. on our servers

Your personal, unshared items — the entries you keep in Calendar, Tasks, Shopping, Reminders, Ideas, Meals, and Capture — are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to our servers unless you take an action that shares them (for example, sharing an event with your Circle or guests).

Information needed for shared and multi-user features — your account and profile, Circles and memberships, shared events and RSVPs, invitations, messages, notifications, and push tokens — is stored on our service infrastructure (provided by Supabase) so it can sync between the people you share with.

How we use information

  • To create and maintain your account and profile.
  • To provide Circles, shared events, RSVPs, invitations, and messaging.
  • To transcribe and categorize your entries when you use voice or AI sorting.
  • To deliver notifications you have enabled.
  • To respond to your support, privacy, and legal requests.
  • To maintain the security and reliable operation of the app.

Service providers

We use a small number of third-party providers to operate Klayr. They process information only to provide their services to us:

  • Supabase — provides our authentication, database, file storage, realtime, and server functions, and is used to send account emails such as sign-up confirmation and password reset.
  • Anthropic — a third-party AI processor that receives transcribed or typed text in order to categorize your entries.
  • Apple — provides push notification delivery, speech recognition, and calendar functionality on your device.

These providers have their own terms and privacy practices governing how they handle information. We do not control, and this policy does not cover, their independent practices.

How information is shared

Information you choose to share is visible to the people you share it with — for example, your Circle members can see shared events and your profile name and photo, and people you message can see your messages. Aside from the service providers described above, we do not share your personal information with third parties.

We do not sell your personal information, we do not share it for targeted advertising, and we do not use it for cross-app tracking. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or security of our users or the service.

Data retention and account deletion

We retain active account information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide Klayr. You can delete your account from within the app (Settings → Delete Account). Deleting your account removes your account and the active data associated with it, such as your profile, memberships, events you own, RSVPs, invitations you created, notifications, push tokens, and messages tied to your account.

After deletion, limited residual copies of some information may remain temporarily in routine service-provider backups or system logs before they are overwritten or expire in the ordinary course. Content you shared with others (for example, a message you sent) may remain visible to those recipients.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Access to stored data is controlled through account authentication and database access rules that limit each user to their own data and the data shared with them, and information is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, however, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

You can:

  • Access and update your profile information within the app.
  • Correct information you have entered by editing it in the app.
  • Delete your account and its active data using in-app account deletion.
  • Control app permissions (microphone, camera, photos, calendar, notifications) in your iOS Settings.
  • Contact us to ask about the information associated with your account.

To make a privacy request, email us at the address below. We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of in those respects. This policy describes the rights and practices we actually support and is not a claim of formal compliance with any specific privacy law.

Children

Klayr is intended for users age 13 and older and is not directed to children under 13. Children under 13 may not create their own accounts. Adults may enter family information that involves children, and are responsible for having the authority to provide that information. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, please contact us so we can address it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the effective date at the top of this page. Your continued use of Klayr after an update means you accept the revised policy.

Contact us

If you have questions or requests about this policy or your information, contact us at Klayrapp@icloud.com.
